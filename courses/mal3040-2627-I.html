MAL3040: Statistics

Autumn 2026–27 (Semester I) · IIT Jodhpur
Program elective for ITEP BSc–BEd (Mathematics Major) students in their 3rd-year

L-T-P (C): 3-1-0 (4)
Prerequisite: Understanding of probability theory

Syllabus

Objectives

The instructor will:

  1. demonstrate the basic principles of data analysis;
  2. discuss from descriptive statistics to inferential statistics;
  3. explore the applications of probability theory for data analysis.

Learning outcomes

The students will be able to:

  1. present data in the form of charts and numbers;
  2. apply useful distributions to a given data set;
  3. use statistical tools for decision-theoretic problems.

Contents

  1. Descriptive Statistics (4 lectures)
    • Classification and tabulation of data, graphical representation
    • Measures of central tendency: mean, median, mode
    • Measures of dispersion: range, variance, standard deviation, coefficient of variation
    • Correlation coefficient
  2. Sampling Distributions (4 lectures)
    • Chi-square, \(t\), and \(F\) distributions
    • Central limit theorem
  3. Statistical Inference (10 lectures)
    • Point estimation, properties of point estimators: unbiasedness, consistency, efficiency, sufficiency
    • Method of moments and maximum likelihood estimation
    • Confidence interval estimation
  4. Hypothesis Testing (10 lectures)
    • Null and alternative hypotheses, Type I and Type II errors, power of a test, \(p\)-values
    • One-sample tests for mean, variance, and proportion
    • Two-sample tests, test for independence, chi-square goodness-of-fit test
  5. Regression and Correlation (8 lectures)
    • Karl Pearson's correlation, rank correlation
    • Simple linear regression and multiple regression
  6. Demonstrations Using R (4 lectures)
