\cvsection{Current Research Directions}

\textbf{Majority coloring game on trees} \\
With Yash Chawda (IIT Jodhpur), Saraswati Girish Nanoti (IISc) and Eshwar Srinivasan (Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham, Coimbatore).
Investigating optimal strategies and computational complexity for the two-player majority coloring game, particularly on trees.
At the MATRIX--MFO Tandem Workshop (2026), also with Stijn Cambie (KU Leuven Kulak), Katie Clinch (University of Queensland), Annika Heckel (Uppsala University), Allan Lo (University of Birmingham), Yelena Yuditsky (University of Leeds) and Gabri\"elle Zwaneveld (University of Amsterdam), studied the majority coloring game on random graphs, cycles, wheels and fans.

\textbf{\(\triangle\)-convexity in graphs} \\
With Vishnu Kumar (IIT Jodhpur). Investigating convexity structures in graphs, particularly computing dimensional parameters such as the exchange number and Carath\'eodory number for \(\triangle\)-convexity, and characterizing the extremal examples.

\textbf{Fractional intersecting families} \\
Continuing investigation of the Balachandran--Mathew--Mishra conjecture on the maximum size of fractional \(\theta\)-intersecting families over \([n]\), building on \href{https://doi.org/10.37236/12900}{Balachandran--Das--Sankarnarayanan (2025)} and \href{https://doi.org/10.37236/11651}{Balachandran--Bhattacharya--Kher--Mathew--Sankarnarayanan (2023)}.

\textbf{List colorings of graphs on surfaces} \\
Investigating the gap between the choice number and the chromatic number for graphs embedded on surfaces, and efficient list coloring algorithms for such graphs, particularly the \(1\)-skeleta of regular maps on closed surfaces, building on \href{https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dam.2026.01.032}{Balachandran--Sankarnarayanan (2026)}, \href{https://doi.org/10.1007/s00026-022-00573-8}{Sankarnarayanan (2022)} and \href{https://doi.org/10.37236/10263}{Balachandran--Sankarnarayanan (2021)}.
